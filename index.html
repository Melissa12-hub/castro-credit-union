Why a credit union

The difference is who the bank works for.

Credit unions are not-for-profit and member-owned. Here's what that changes in practice.

Lower loan rates

Without shareholders to pay, savings on auto and personal loans get passed to members instead.

Fewer, clearer fees

Checking accounts carry no monthly fee. What you see when you open the account is what you pay.

A vote, not just an account

Members elect the volunteer board that sets policy — one member, one vote, no matter the balance.

Money stays local

Deposits are lent back out to members in the same community, not routed to distant shareholders.
